# Design Guidance

[DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) is the authoritative project design language. Use the [design workflow](../workflows/design-development.md) when establishing or revising it.

Keep supporting examples, component notes, and review artifacts here when they are intentionally shared. Private captures and draft evidence belong in ignored output folders.

For complex or reused UI, prefer examples that make important states easy to inspect: a story, direct route, fixture, rendered artifact, or small test surface. Choose the simplest observation path that demonstrates the change; creating a separate showcase is optional.

Review representative results against the project design decisions, accessibility requirements, and user-visible behavior. Promote reusable decisions back to DESIGN.md rather than copying its rules into this directory's index.
